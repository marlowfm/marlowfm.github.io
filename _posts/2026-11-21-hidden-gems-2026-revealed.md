Marlow's Hidden Gems of 2026 have been revealed. The winners were announced at an awards ceremony and party held at Harleyford Golf Club on Saturday 21st November.

![Marlow's 2026 Hidden Gems revealed]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/logos/hidden-gems-2026-logo.jpg "Marlow's 2026 Hidden Gems revealed")

Congratulations to the winners below, but also to every person, business, and organisation nominated. Marlow is so lucky to have you all!

- **Best Cuppa** - The M Café
- **Best Breakfast** - Fego
- **Best Event** - Marlow Town Regatta & Festival
- **Best Sports Club** - Marlow Rowing Club
- **Best Independent Small Business** - Chives
- **Best Customer Service - Hospitality** - The Barn Club
- **Best Customer Service - Retail** - The Meat Hook of Marlow
- **Services to Charity** - ???
- **Services to the Community** - ???

More than 6,600 votes were cast when we announced the shortlists of nominees, after the public put forward nominations for each category.

Money raised from the event will go to help keep Marlow FM on air and serving the community.

Our new fundraiser, _Style a Tile_, is now open, and we'd love you to be part of it. For £19.75, you can design your own 10cm square ceramic tile, and we'll put it up on the wall in the Marlow FM kitchen.

![Style a Tile]({{ site.url }}{{ site.baseurl }}/assets/images/news/style-a-tile.png "Style a Tile")

It couldn't be simpler. [Buy a tile](https://tiles.marlowfm.co.uk){:target="\_blank"} and we'll email you a tile code straight away. Then design your tile online - on your phone or computer - whenever you like. Use a favourite photo, a message, a drawing by the kids, or anything else that means something to you.

We'll check each design, have it printed on ceramic, and fix it to the wall, and we'll email you a photo once it's up. We plan to put the first tiles up early next year, and we'll have events during 2027 when you'll be able to come and see your tile in person.

It also makes a lovely gift, just in time for Christmas. Buy a tile for someone else, and you can make a printable gift voucher with the code on it, ready to pop in a card. They can then design and submit their own tile.

Every tile helps to keep Marlow FM on the air, so you'll be supporting your community radio station and we'll see your design every time we put the kettle on!

[Buy your tile now!](https://tiles.marlowfm.co.uk){:target="\_blank"}

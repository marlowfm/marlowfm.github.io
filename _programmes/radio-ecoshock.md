Every Wednesday from 9pm to 10pm.

Alex Smith explores the latest science, ideas, and solutions shaping our world. Featuring interviews with leading scientists, authors, and activists, the show covers climate change, oceans, forests, pollution, energy, economics, peace, and other critical environmental issues in an engaging and accessible format.
